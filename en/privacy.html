Privacy policy

Steep Notes is a local application: it runs on your Mac and has no servers. This page explains what it does with your Google data.

Who provides it

Steep Notes is a personal project. Contact: open an issue at github.com/labombina7/steep-notes-pages/issues.

Which Google data it uses and why

When you connect a Google account, Steep Notes asks for these permissions:

  • openid and email: to know which account you connected and avoid mixing accounts (for example, your personal and your work account).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive (Google Drive): to read the files in the folders you choose as input (including Google Docs, Sheets and Slides, which are exported as text) and to write the results to the folder you choose as output. Steep Notes only accesses the folders you configure in your flows.

Where your data goes

  • Files are downloaded from your Google Drive to your Mac, and results are uploaded from your Mac to your Drive.
  • If a flow uses AI, the text of each file is sent to your own Claude account (Anthropic) together with your instructions. No other service receives your data.
  • Steep Notes has no servers, collects no usage analytics or telemetry, and does not sell or share data with anyone.
  • Google's authorization (the refresh token) is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. Application logs contain neither document content nor credentials.

Limited Use

Steep Notes' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is only used to provide the features you see in the app, never for advertising or to train AI models.

How to revoke access

  • In Steep Notes: Connections → Remove deletes the authorization from the Keychain.
  • In your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions → "Steep Notes" → "Remove access".
  • The results Steep Notes saved on your Mac or in your Drive are yours: delete them whenever you like.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Last updated: 22 October 2026.
