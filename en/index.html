Your documents, processed with AI on your own Mac

Steep Notes watches a folder (local or in Google Drive), processes every new file once with the AI and instructions you choose, and saves the result wherever you want.

How it works

  • Source: a folder on your Mac or a folder in your Google Drive (for example, your Meet meeting notes).
  • Processing: audio is transcribed on your Mac; then your own Claude account applies your instructions.
  • Result: one Markdown document per file in the folder you choose, including Google Drive.

Local, no servers

Steep Notes has no servers of its own and collects no analytics. Your files only travel from your Drive to your Mac and, if the flow uses AI, to your Claude account. Credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain.

Google Drive

To read and write in Drive, Steep Notes asks for permission on Google's consent screen. Because it is a small project, Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app": you can continue with "Advanced → Go to Steep Notes". What data it uses and how to revoke access: privacy policy.
